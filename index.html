PDF Easy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

PDF Easy is a PDF viewer and editor for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your device. PDF Easy collects no personal data, has no accounts, shows no ads and has no internet access.

Information we collect

None. PDF Easy does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. It contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information in its private storage on your device. Other apps can't read it:

  • Your documents. PDFs you open are read through Android's file picker or shared from other apps. PDF Easy only accesses the files you choose. Files shared to the app are copied into its private storage so they can be opened again. Temporary copies and page thumbnails are kept in the app's cache while you work.
  • Saved signatures. Signatures you draw are encrypted with a key stored in your device's Android Keystore. The key never leaves your device.
  • Recent documents. The names and locations of files you opened recently, so you can find them again.
  • Settings. Your appearance choices, such as theme and colours.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers.

Your control

  • Delete individual signatures from Manage signatures.
  • Empty the recent documents list with Clear history, and remove thumbnails and temporary files with Clear cache, both in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data it stored. Your own PDF files are never deleted by the app.

Sharing

A document is sent to another app only when you choose Share. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you pick.

Children

PDF Easy collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
